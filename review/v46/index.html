What the evidence covers

The videos capture the game’s WebGPU canvas at 1440 × 900 with its audio stream, using native key and mouse events and a stationary camera in Fly mode. The separate text overlay is visible in screenshots but is outside the video stream. The three-angle stills use a test-only viewmodel rotation and closer offset to expose grip seams. The only change after these recordings keeps stationary Settings/About text visible; weapon code, shaders, physics and assets are identical.

Barrel separation, barrel stacking and two-barrel magnetic capture are exercised by the GPU gauntlet. The release receipt records the shader, gameplay and native-resolution performance checks.

Startup remains unfinished: mesh generation now runs in a worker and is cached. The loader updates its actual stage, elapsed time and shader count. Isolated Firefox measured about 22 seconds cold and 10 seconds cached before the entry button; shader compilation remains the main delay.

The room also gains dry surface grain and LTC response. Barlow SDF glyphs cover in-game labels and interface text, including the curved bat label. Loading and error messages use browser text until the GPU is available.
