# SMEAR performance

V18 / 0.18.0 keeps approximately 60 FPS at 3000x1800 in hardware Chrome and Firefox across idle, pistol, Spill, drag and Chaos. Chrome CPU p99 is 1.6 to 1.8 ms, compute p99 is 1.38 to 2.82 ms and render p99 is 3.08 to 3.74 ms. Firefox CPU p99 is 1 to 2 ms, compute p99 is 1.69 to 3.13 ms and render p99 is 2.89 to 3.91 ms. End-to-end tick rates are 119.2 to 119.5 in Chrome and 116.0 to 116.5 in Firefox, including harness inspection overhead. These runs are presentation-capped and separate from recording, with active audio and muted browser output.

All 90 wall-impact, liquid, wet-contact, gameplay and native-profile checks pass. The matched articulated wall slam leaves 2.26 times as many opaque pigment pixels as V17 and spreads across 1.51 metres instead of 0.84 metres. Its central pigment is retained after 15 seconds. The isolated wall film descends 53.83 cm in three seconds, versus 98.76 cm in V17, and still supplies the floor within the revised 30-second runoff window. The matched floor impact has identical pigment and liquid totals. No new GPU buffers or normal-play body/paint readbacks were added. See [the V18 receipt](docs/qa/wall-v18.json) for measurements and the matched wall-slam/Spill MP4.

V17 / 0.17.0 keeps approximately 60 FPS at 3000x1800 across idle, pistol, Spill, drag and Chaos in hardware Chrome and Firefox. Chrome CPU p99 is 1.5 to 1.6 ms, compute p99 is 1.51 to 2.95 ms and render p99 is 2.88 to 3.34 ms. Firefox CPU p99 is 2 ms, compute p99 is 1.54 to 2.68 ms and render p99 is 2.78 to 3.11 ms. End-to-end simulation rates are 119.3 to 119.5 ticks/second in Chrome and 115.8 to 116.5 in Firefox, including harness inspection overhead. These are presentation-capped runs, measured separately from recording with audio running and browser output muted.

Persistent film velocity adds 8.10 MiB over V16. The film uses bounded 1/60-second substeps, plus an odd 1/120-second final tick; the rig remains at 120 Hz. Both browsers pass gameplay, native profiling, nine wet-contact cases and thirteen liquid/gravity cases (78 checks). Normal-play audits contain no body or pigment downloads, canvas reads or WebGL contexts. The finite contact fixture shifts the liquid centroid 13.19 cm, versus 5.82 cm in V16 (2.27 times farther). Wall liquid descends 98.76 cm in three seconds; 57.6% reaches the floor after fifteen seconds. Elevated-edge runoff conserves surface plus airborne supply within 0.3% in its six-second fixture, and a full 900-drop pool retains un-emitted liquid. See [the V17 receipt](docs/qa/gravity-v17.json) for measurements, scope and matched MP4s.
